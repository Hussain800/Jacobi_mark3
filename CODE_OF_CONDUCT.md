# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, harassment-free community for contributors and users of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, retailer/platform boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Admit uncertainty and correct mistakes without blame.
- Give credit and preserve authorship.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and user safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks;
- publishing private information or credentials;
- intimidation, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation;
- encouraging unauthorized access, evasion, fraud, or deceptive savings claims;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their verified GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants according to severity and context.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere. Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and provide a fair opportunity to respond where safe.
